# AI se Fast Seekhne ka System

## Daily routine (2.5 ghante)
1. **15 min** – Kal ka revision: AI se 5 quiz questions maango.
2. **60 min** – Aaj ka topic video (Jeremy's IT Lab) + AI se saath saath doubts.
3. **60 min** – Lab Packet Tracer mein khud karo. Stuck ho to AI ko config + error do.
4. **15 min** – Notes: AI se summary banwao, apni zaban mein likho.

## Golden rules
- AI ko tutor banao, answer machine nahi. Pehle khud try, phir poochho.
- Har config khud type karo. Copy-paste se skill nahi aati.
- Har concept ko khud explain karo ("Feynman"): AI se kaho "main samjhata hoon, galti pakdo".
- Har hafte mock quiz. Jo galat ho wo notebook mein.

## Ready-made prompts
**Teacher**
> Tum CCNA instructor ho. Mujhe [topic] Roman Urdu mein simple examples se samjhao, phir 5 MCQ poochho, ek ek karke.

**Lab banao**
> Mujhe [topic] ka Packet Tracer lab do: topology, IP table, steps, aur end mein verification commands. Solution mat batao jab tak main na kahun.

**Troubleshoot**
> Yeh meri config hai [paste]. Ping fail ho raha hai. Seedha jawab mat do, pehle mujhe 3 show commands batao jo main chalaun.

**Subnetting drill**
> Mujhe 10 random subnetting questions do (difficulty badhta jaye). Main jawab dunga, tum check karna.

**Mock exam**
> CCNA 200-301 style 20 questions do [topic list] se, exam ki tarah. Ant mein score aur weak areas batao.

**Revision cards**
> [topic] ke 15 flashcards banao (Q/A format) jo Anki mein import ho sakein.

**Explain error**
> Is show command output ko line by line samjhao: [paste]

## Tools
- Claude / ChatGPT: tutor
- Anki: flashcards (spaced repetition)
- Packet Tracer / GNS3: labs
- Wireshark: packet dekhna
- GitHub: labs aur notes upload (portfolio proof)
